Final project · Ironhack

Is memory loss only about age?

I used to work with people with dementia, so I wanted to understand memory loss better.

Question
Is memory loss only about getting older?
What I did
Studied two large US health surveys, then built a model that helps a health team choose who to contact first.
Result
No. People aged 45 to 64 report memory loss almost as often as people over 65. Low income, depression and hearing problems matter too.

Vanguard website test

Question
Does a new website design help clients finish their online process?
What I did
Cleaned and checked the data of 70,609 clients, then compared the old and new design with 50,500 clients.
Result
More clients finished: 69.3% instead of 65.6%. But the gain is not proven to reach the 5% target, so the advice was to fix the first step before launch.
