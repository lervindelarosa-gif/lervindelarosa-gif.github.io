Frost brings the most extra disruptions

Disruptions on a typical day (median), by weather at KNMI De Bilt · all years 2019–2025 (not affected by the year filter)

Show data

Longer, not just more. There are only 12% more disruptions than in 2019, but the hours lost almost doubled. A typical disruption is only a bit longer; disruptions of more than 8 hours happen 2.6× as often.

2023 was the worst year. Mostly strikes at regional train companies such as Arriva and Keolis early in the year, plus staff shortages.

Infrastructure is the biggest cause overall, ahead of rolling stock (broken trains) and staff.

Weather matters only when it is extreme. Leaf fall (herfstblad) is small; hard frost adds the most disruptions (+57% on a typical day).
