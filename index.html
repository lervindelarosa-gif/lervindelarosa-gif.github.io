Final project · Ironhack

Is memory loss only about age?

I used to work with people with dementia, so I wanted to understand memory loss better.

Question
Is memory loss only about getting older?
What I did
Studied two large US health surveys, then built a model that helps a health team choose who to contact first.
Result
No. People aged 45 to 64 report memory loss almost as often as people over 65. Low income, depression and hearing problems matter too.

Personal project · Power BI

What disrupts Dutch trains?

Question
Are train disruptions in the Netherlands getting worse, and what causes them?
What I did
Loaded 38,571 disruptions from 2019 to 2025 into MySQL, then built a Power BI dashboard with DAX measures and a year filter.
Result
There are only 12% more disruptions, but the total disruption time almost doubled. A normal disruption is only a bit longer. The big change is the very long ones: disruptions of more than 8 hours happen 2.6 times as often. 2023 was the worst year, mostly because of staff problems.

Machine learning · Ironhack

Star, galaxy or quasar?

Question
Can a model tell what a telescope is looking at: a star, a galaxy or a quasar?
What I did
Cleaned 100,000 sky objects, added colour features, and compared 5 models.
Result
The best model is right 98% of the time. With cheap colour data only, it still reaches 88.6%, so a survey could save expensive measurements for the objects that matter.
